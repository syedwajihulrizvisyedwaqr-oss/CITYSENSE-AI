# 🌆 CITYSENSE AI — The City That Sees Itself

> AI-powered urban intelligence for safer, more accessible and more responsive cities.

## 🚀 Overview

CITYSENSE AI is a civic-tech platform powered by Google Gemini that transforms citizen-submitted images into actionable urban intelligence.

It combines three core innovations:

### 🔎 AI Problem Hunter
Uses Gemini's multimodal capabilities to analyze real-world urban images and identify problems such as potholes, damaged sidewalks, waste, lighting issues and other infrastructure concerns.

### ♿ Invisible Accessibility
Detects accessibility barriers that traditional city monitoring may miss, including blocked sidewalks, missing ramps, stairs, obstacles and unsafe pedestrian routes.

### 🌐 AI City Twin
Transforms detected issues into a visual digital representation of the city, allowing planners to understand problem locations, severity, accessibility impact and priority actions.
